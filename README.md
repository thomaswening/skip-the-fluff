# Skip the Fluff

An unofficial, distraction-free wrapper for duolingo.com on Android.

Skip the Fluff shows the Duolingo website in an Android app and leaves out the screens that do not serve learning: "use the app" prompts, leaderboard popups, streak nags, upsells, celebration animations and lesson intros. A lesson then takes little more time than its exercises do. It keeps the whole screen for the lesson and keeps your place when you switch apps briefly.

It does **not** answer, skip or complete exercises. It does not change XP, gems or streaks, does not unlock paid features and does not modify Duolingo's own app. The full list is in [REQ-003](docs/requirements.md#req-003).

## Disclaimer

Skip the Fluff is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Duolingo. You use it at your own risk.

## Installing

Not yet possible. Once there is a first release, you download its APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/thomaswening/skip-the-fluff/releases) on your phone and install it. You need no computer, root or developer options for this.

## Reporting a fix that stopped working

Duolingo can change its website at any time. A distraction can then come back, or Skip the Fluff may hide something it should not. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/thomaswening/skip-the-fluff/issues) that names the screen, includes a screenshot and gives the app version from the about screen.
